Modern Construction Technologies

1. Construction technologies will shape the industry in the future.Construction technology is probably one of the most important things about the future in construction.

Construction companies, universities and technology companies are working hard to solve the problem in the industry like safety issues and the skilled labor shortage.

Companies are finding ways to improve productivity, reduce workplace injuries and push construction into the future.

These new construction technologies are shaping the industry now  and the changes made will leave the industry completely changed.

2. AI & Machine learning

AI and machine learning sound like something from a sci-fi and their place in construction sound complicated and improbable. However, AI and machine learning are a part of a trend that will shape the industry for years to come.

Companies now are using machine learning to improve job site safety. Smartvid.io is one such company. It collects visual information from cameras to reduce risk.

Using field observations and automatic observations by their AI, their platform can warn managers of potential risks and hazards on the site.

Artificial intelligence and machine learning could come to play an even bigger part in the construction industry.

Areas like project management could soon see AI aid.

Artificial intelligence can eventually be used to help in many different areas in construction, and with more users, the predictive abilities will get better.

3. Augmented Reality and Virtual Reality

With the help of augmented reality, a combination of virtual reality and reality, construction technology experts are predicting that that will be the game-changer.

The physical technology as well as the software needed in order to use and operate augmented reality is expensive still.

While there are companies working to make it cheaper and more accessible to a wider range of consumers, now it is mostly used by very large and wealthy companies.

Companies using augmented reality are seeing the benefits.

4. Autonomous Equipment

Some companies are starting to build their own autonomous vehicles for the job site.

Boston Dynamics has built its own semi-autonomous robot. They can be used on the job site to survey. Some companies eventually use tech to ensure workplace safety alongside human professionals.

Komatsu, a heavy-equipment manufacturer, is additionally working on autonomous construction vehicles.

5. Drones

Drone usage is increasing on construction sites and used for a variety of purposes.

One of the common usages of this construction technology is for surveying land.

Drones can decrease the amount of time needed to produce an accurate survey.

They also eliminate some of the human error in the process.

The real-time views have also been used for improving job site safety. The drone footage can be reviewed without any person actually having to go to a potentially hazardous area.

This reduces the number of workplace injuries and improves workplace safety.

6. Robots

In construction technology, robots are gaining in popularity. From the brick laying robots to the 3D concrete printing robots, there is a drive to build the next great thing. These robots do repetitive motions or they work as aids. The MULE (Material Unit Lift Enhancer) helps workers load and place heavy material. This robot doesn’t replace but supplement human laborers. And by using a MULE, itsaves workers the repetitive injury strain they face.

And independent robots won’t be the only technology seen on a job site. Several companies have started developing exoskeletons for commercial use. These would help reduce fatigue for field workers which could reduce the number of job site injuries.

7. Modular Construction

Modular construction means that the project is constructed entirely or almost entirely off-site and assembled on site. Marriott International has been pioneering this in their hotel construction for the past several years. They announced originally that they were going to build roughly 50 hotels using modular construction. This year they announced they were going to build a 360-foot-tall modular hotel in 90 days.While an entire hotel in 90 days sounds improbable, modular construction makes it possible.
