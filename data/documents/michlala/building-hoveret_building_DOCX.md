Building material is any material which is used for construction purposes. Many natural substances, such as clay, rocks, sand and wood, even twigs and leaves, are used to construct buildings. Apart from natural materials, many man-made products are in use, some more and some less synthetic.

One of the most popular building materials is concrete. It is a building material made from the combination of aggregate and a binder such as cement. The most common form of concrete is Portland cement concrete, which consists of gravel and sand,cement and water.  Concrete has been the major building material in the modern age due to its longevity, formability, and ease of transport.

Metal is used as structural framework for larger buildings such as skyscrapers, or as an external surface covering. There are many types of metals used for building. Steel is a metal alloy whose major component is iron, and is the usual choice for metal structural building materials. It is strong, flexible, and if refined well and lasts a long time.

It requires a great deal of human labor to produce metal, especially in the large amounts needed for the building industries.

The metal's prime enemy when it comes to longevity is corrosion.

Blocks and bricks

Blocks are usually larger than bricks. Bricks are usually portable and you can use one hand to lift it. However, the block usually requires both hands to lift it and can be laid more quickly than bricks. But, bigger size also means less versatility in laying especially when building up ends.  Generally blocks are plastered.

Clay blocks:

They are usually  hollow units. The material used in their manufacturing is the same as bricks. After firing, clay blocks are dense, hard and brittle (easy to crack) which make them difficult to cut and fix.

Concrete blocks:

Concrete blocks are also known as concrete masonry units. Concrete masonry walls, correctly designed and constructed will satisfy a variety of building requirements including fire resistance, durability, aesthetics and acoustics.

Concrete Lightweight Blocks

The advantage of lightweight blocks are

Easier to handle and quicker to lay

The air in lightweight block provide better sound and thermal insulation

The lighter weight cause in lighter foundations and structural members

Can be cut with hand tools and hold nails and screws without plugs.

Concrete blocks can be manufactured from either natural aggregates or lightweight aggregates.

Aerated concrete is unlike traditional concrete. They have good thermal insulation properties but lower sound insulation than concrete units. They also have lower strength and need to be handled more carefully. However, they are much easier to cut and shape.

Thermal and sound insulation: thermal conductivity of block varies with density. However, the lightweight concrete blocks have better thermal insulation compared to those denser block.

Fire resistance:

Concrete blocks have a good fire resistance.

The building and construction industry is marked by a high rate of serious work accidents. Read what you can do to improve safety as an employer or employee.

In order to prevent work accidents and other health problems among the employees, it is important, that both employers and workers contribute to ensuring the safety of their working environment. The employer bears overall responsibility for the working environment but the employees share responsibility; to follow employer directions, for example and use the personal protective equipment the employer has provided.

The building and construction industry has a high number of work accidents compared to other industries. The most common accidents in the building and construction industry are:

Falls from height, from roofs, scaffolding or ladders.

Falls at ground level, when walking on slippery or uneven surfaces.

Accidents with power tools or machines, for example with chainsaws.

Heavy lifting accidents.

Employers’ obligations:

All employers who are obliged to give their employees adequate training and instruction so that work can be performed safely and securely.

Engineering Materials

All engineering materials are divided into metals and non-metals.

Copper, cast iron, aluminium are examples of metals. Rubber, plastic

and ceramics are examples of non-metals. Today different metals are

widely used in machine-building industry.

We can divide all metals into ferrous and non-ferrous. Steel and

cast iron are in the group of ferrous metals. They are alloys of iron with

carbon, manganese, silicon and other components.

Non-ferrous metals are metals and alloys the main component of

which is not iron but some other element such as aluminium, copper and

others. Some of the characteristics of non-ferrous metals are high electric and heat conductivity, high corrosion resistance and light weight.

Alloys

The most useful metals are irons, copper and aluminum. However, only small quantities of these materials are employed in their pure form. Most of useful metallic materials are formed from a combination of the above metals, known as alloys. An alloy is a mixture of two or more materials melted together. For making alloys, metals are usually melted in their liquid state. A steel containing some metallic elements other than iron and carbon is usually known as "special steel". These various metals when added to steel increase the hardness of the steel.

Some alloys occur naturally, such as electrum, which is an alloy that is native to Earth, consisting of silver and gold. Meteorites are sometimes made of natural alloys of iron and nickel. One of the first alloys made by humans was bronze which is made by mixing the metals tin and copper. Bronze was an extremely useful alloy to the ancients, because it is much stronger and harder than either of its components. Steel was another common alloy.

Essential construction tools for beginners fall under three categories: general tools, wood tools, and masonry/concrete tools. All beginners should have general tools, no matter which material they are planning to work with. Those planning to work with wood should purchase wood tools and those planning to work with stone and concrete should purchase masonry/concrete tools. All of the tools in each category are highly useful and most are very portable.

General Tools

General tools are very useful. Beginners should therefore start building a collection with these tools since they will find them in their hands frequently.

Measuring Tool: they  include tape measures and laser meters.

Tape measure

Laser meter

Tape measures are rulers made of thin metal that are capable of measuring objects and distances of 100 feet in length. Beginners may not need tape which is that long, so there are many different options as far as maximum length goes

Laser meters are even more convenient to use because they use a laser beam to calculate lengths. They tend to be more expensive than tape measures are, but the price can still be reasonable.

2. Clamps

Clamps are relatively simple tools, but they are very different and useful. Beginners can use them to hold glued pieces of wood together while the glue dries or holding a piece of concrete or stone in place. Clamps are extra hands and extra hands are always useful on a construction job. Moreover, they are very strong hands, capable of holding objects where human hands would fail.

Knives and Cutters

Cutter

All beginners should have some kind of knife or cutter.. Utility knives are strong, sharp, and useful in situations when you need a heavy-duty blade. Swiss Army knives are more versatile, having numerous small tools in a single handle. Choosing one or another type of knife depends on what the project calls for. Swiss Army knives are better suited towards highly detailed work and utility knives for situations that call for a strong, sharp blade.

Air Compressor

An air compressor is necessary for any beginner who plans to use a pneumatic tool, and beginners should have at least one such tool in their collection. Air compressors compress air and deliver it as a power source for pneumatic tools. Compressed air is a more powerful source of energy than humans can generate on their own. Pneumatic tools can help construction workers complete jobs more quickly than they would be able to with manually operated tools. There are some cases where a construction worker cannot complete a task without the power of compressed air.

Hammer

Hammers serve many purposes that make them suitable for various kinds of construction work. Carpenter hammers are designed to drive nails into wood and remove them.

Mason hammers are designed to break up bricks and stonework.

Those who are planning to work with wood should buy tools that are designed for use with wood. Beginners should find that they use these tools quite often.

6. Screwdriver

Screwdrivers help construction workers place screws into wood and to remove them as well. It is possible to buy sets of screwdrivers that work with different screw sizes. Another option is to buy a handle with interchangeable heads that work with different screw sizes and different forms, such as Phillips head and flat head screws.

7. Drill

A drill is a versatile tool because the user can change bits to perform different tasks. The primary job of a drill is to create holes and different bit sizes drill holes of different sizes. With the correct attachment, a drill can also drive and remove screws. It uses an electric-powered motor, so it completes these tasks more quickly than a screwdriver can. However, it can also cause damage to the head of the screw if the drill bit is not securely in the socket. This makes the screw unusable and may make it difficult to remove the screw from a piece of wood.

8. Circular Saw

A circular saw may not be the smallest tool, but it is still easily portable. It is a powerful device, able to cut through very thick pieces of wood. A circular saw can also be dangerous if the operator is unsure of how to use it, so beginners should make sure that they are familiar with circular saws before going out to buy  one. Those considering purchasing used circular saws should also be sure that the blade is still sharp if they do not plan to replace it.

10. Air Nailer

If a hammer cannot finish a job quickly enough, an air nailer should be handy for more efficient completion of the task. This tool uses compressed air to drive nails, saving both time and energy that is necessary to use a hammer to do the same job manually. Air nailers generally cost more than hammers, but they save time.

Masonry/Concrete Tools

Wood tools have some application outside of working with wood, but masonry/concrete tools tend to be more specialized. Here are some of concrete tools that you may find useful

Mixers

Mixers are large tools, but they are still portable if a mason has a truck. They are important for anyone working with stone or concrete because they quickly and automatically mix concrete and mortar, saving time and energy over doing these tasks manually. Mixers run off electricity or gasoline.

Masonry Saw

A masonry saw is useful for cutting ceramic pieces. It has a diamond blade that users can operate at waist-level. Masonry saws are powerful devices, so users should be sure that they know how to operate them before buying one.

15. Impact Wrench פטיש אוויר

An impact wrench usually uses compressed air to generate the power that is necessary to drill concrete. It uses the same mechanics as a hammer. But it is powerful enough to drill and place screws into concrete where hand tools would fail. This may be the most specialized of concrete/masonry tools, but beginners may quickly discover that is handy to have one available.

Conclusion

Beginners may find that they are able to find work in the construction industry with little experience. What they do need, however, are the right tools for the job. Selecting construction tools can be a challenge for beginners, but with careful consideration they can find  useful tools without spending a fortune.

Beginners should know what kind of material, such as wood, stone, or concrete, with which they plan to work, how useful tools are for the jobs that they plan to work, and how portable tools are. Taking these factors into account, beginners will find a group of tools that will allow them perform many necessary tasks on a job site.

There are a few general tools that all beginners should have no matter what kind of work they plan to do and tools that are specific to certain tasks.

MSDS

A material safety data sheet (MSDS) (also known as PSDS (Product safety data sheet) is a form with data regarding the properties of a particular substance.

It provides workers and emergency personnel with procedures for handling or working with that substance in a safe manner, and includes information such as physical data (melting point,boiling point etc.), toxicity, health effects, first aid, reactivity, storage, disposal, protective equipment, and spill-handling procedures.

MSDS formats can vary from source to source within a country depending on national requirements.

MSDS (material safety data sheets) are a widely used system for cataloging information on chemicals and chemical mixtures. MSDS information may include instructions for the safe use and potential hazards associated with a particular material or product. These data sheets can be found anywhere where chemicals are being used.

There is also a duty to properly label substances on the basis of physico-chemical, health and/or environmental risk.

Labels can include hazard symbols such as the European Union standard black diagonal cross on an orange background, used to denote a harmful substance.

An MSDS for a substance is not primarily intended for use by the general consumer, focusing instead on the hazards of working with the material in a working environment.

In some cases the MSDS is required to state the chemical's risks, safety, and effect on the environment.

It is important to use an MSDS specific to both country and supplier, as the same product can have different formulations in different countries. The formulation and hazard of a product using a generic name (e.g. sugar soap) may vary between manufacturers in the same country.

Machine-building materials:

Different materials are widely used in the machine-building industry. Materials applied for industrial purposes are called "engineering metals".  There are two groups of metals: ferrous metals and non-ferrous metals. Non-ferrous metals are more expensive than ferrous metals. The metals most frequently used to make non-ferrous metal castings are copper, tin, zinc, nickel, gold and aluminum. Ferrous metals consist of iron combined with carbon, silicon, phosphorus and other elements. Carbon is the most important element in ferrous alloys.

Ferrous metals are used in industry in two general forms: steel and cast iron, which differ in the quantity of carbon content. These two ferrous alloys are derived from pig iron which is produced in a blast furnace. Metals are usually melted and poured into a form which is called "a mould". This process is known as casting. The work of casting metals is performed in foundries.
