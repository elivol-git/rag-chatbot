[Slide 1] 1905-1962
DOV KARMI

[Slide 2] BASIC FACTS 

During his professional career he designed more than two hundred buildings, mostly in Tel Aviv .
Karmi's main style was modernist; he influenced a generation of Israeli architects.
 Israel Prize
In 1957, Karmi was awarded the Israel Prize, for architecture, the first recipient of the Prize in this field.
Strongly influenced by Le Corbusier
He was one of a group of architects who developed a unique Bauhaus style for Tel Aviv, later recognized by UNESCO as a world heritage site. He emphasized simplicity and functionalism.

[Slide 6] In 1949 Karmi won the bid to plan the Histadrut buildings on Arlosoroff Street in Tel Aviv. Ironically, the "bourgeois" architect who was not a member of the Histadrut, was the one chosen to design its symbol. The Histadrut complex of buildings are among the hallmarks of 1950s architecture - visual representations of Israeli sovereignty and of the welfare state, and among the most successful office buildings in the country to this day.
