[Slide 1] Baroque
Baroque architecture flourished between the late 16th and mid-18th century. The architectural style which emerged in Italy soon spread to the rest of Europe and by the 17th century, Spanish Baroque style reached Latin America. It used to express the triumph of the Roman Catholic Church and  a visual demonstration of monarchies in the form of magnificent palaces.

[Slide 2] San Carlo alle Quattro Fontane, Rome
Designed by one of the leading Baroque architects Francesco Borromini ,is one of the finest examples of Baroque architecture. It is the Roman Catholic church that was built between 1638 and 1646.

[Slide 3] Les Invalides, Paris
Les Invalides is one of the greatest masterpieces of French Baroque architecture. Built as a retirement home and hospital for veterans between 1671 and 1678, Les Invalides is today home to the museum of the French Army but it is also the final resting place of several French national heroes including Napoleon Bonaparte.

[Slide 4] Palace of Versailles, Versailles
The Palace of Versailles, one of the grandest palaces ever built is the finest example of secular Baroque architecture. Since 1837, the Palace of Versailles is open to the public as a museum.

[Slide 5] Karlskirche, Vienna
The beautiful Baroque church is one of Vienna’s most admired buildings. It was commissioned by the Emperor Charles VI (1685-1740) who vowed to build a church after the last major plague outbreak in the early 18th century.

[Slide 6] St Paul's Cathedral, London
Built on a site of an earlier church that was severely damaged in the Great Fire of London in 1666, the St Paul’s Cathedral is widely considered as one of the finest examples of English Baroque architecture. From 1710 when completed until 1962, St Paul’s was the tallest building in London.

[Slide 7] Winter Palace, Saint Petersburg
The Winter Palace was built as the residence of Peter the Great (1672-1725) in Saint Petersburg and served as the official residence of the Romanov monarchs from 1732 until the Russian Revolution of 1917. Peter’s palace which was designed by Swiss Italian architect Domenico Trezzini .
